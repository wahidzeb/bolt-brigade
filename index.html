About Bolt Brigade

Bolt Brigade is a pop-out-and-duck blaster. You hide behind toy blocks and zap wind-up robots across 35 missions and five stages, each with a boss at the end.

On a phone you touch the screen to aim and your blaster fires by itself; hold the blue COVER button to duck and reload. Coins buy new blasters and gear in the Armory. The trick is not shooting more, it is ducking at the right moment.

3 quick tips

  1. Duck straight away when you see a red ! above a bot, especially two at once.
  2. Shoot grenades and rockets out of the air. It saves you a hit.
  3. Reload behind cover. Holding COVER refills your magazine, so duck before you are empty.

How to play

📱 Phone: touch to aim, your blaster fires by itself. Hold 🛡 COVER to duck and reload.

🖱 Computer: hold the mouse or Space to pop out of cover. Hold C or Shift to stay in cover.

↔ Drag while holding to aim. Aim assist snaps to bots for you.

✋ Let go to duck and reload. Duck when a bot shows a red !

💥 Shoot grenades and rockets out of the air, smash crates and blow up ⚡ barrels.

⚡ EMP charges as you hit bots. Tap it to zap everything!

👑 Bosses open their glowing cores after they attack: that's when to strike.

On a computer: mouse to aim, hold click or Space to pop out, E for EMP.
